## Videos

- [Let's build GPT: from scratch, in code, spelled out. — Andrej Karpathy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kCc8FmEb1nY) — the single best way to internalize what a parameter is, what a forward pass does, and why generation is one token at a time. Builds a GPT in ~200 lines of PyTorch. Watch before reading `model-architecture-basics.md` if you can.
- [[1hr Talk] Intro to Large Language Models — Andrej Karpathy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zjkBMFhNj_g) — broad overview: tokens, embeddings, next-token prediction, fine-tuning. Good first watch for the whole topic 00.
- [Attention in transformers, step-by-step | Deep Learning Chapter 6 — 3Blue1Brown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMlx5fFNoYc) — the clearest visual explanation of Q/K/V and multi-head attention. Pairs with `model-architecture-basics.md`.
- [State of GPT | BRK216HFS — Microsoft Developer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZQun8Y4L2A) — Karpathy at Microsoft Build: GPT training pipeline, tokenization, scaling. Older but the tokenization and scaling sections still hold up.
